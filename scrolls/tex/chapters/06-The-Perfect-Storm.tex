\chroniclersubsubsection{Closing of the Scroll}\label{performing_arts-closing-of-the-scroll}

\emph{What the Wind Did Not Carry Away}

\emph{(The CHRONICLER returns, hunched, his scroll now damp and frayed. His hat is gone. His sleeves hang loose like flags left after battle. His voice is softer now---like someone recounting loss by lantern light.)}

\chronicler{CHRONICLER:}\\
It hath passed.

Not the storm.\\
But the moment.

The system yet liveth---barely.\\
The tower still stands---but cracked.\\
The mind behind it?\\
Shaken.

\textbf{Tuxicles} hath learned that perfection protecteth naught.\\
That beauty doth not brace.\\
That clarity alone cannot hold\\
against chaos well-fed.

And so now\ldots{} he wonders.

Not how to rise.\\
But how to \emph{survive}.

\emph{The CHRONICLER kneels, smoothing the scroll with tender hands.}

What endureth in the storm?\\
Not structure.\\
Not speed.

Only that which bendeth\\
without forgetting its form.

And the next scroll---\\
ah, the next speaks not of wind\ldots{}\\
but of \textbf{wound}.

For something within Tuxrates hath cracked.\\
And what bleeds from pride,\\
bleeds long.

\emph{He gathers what remains of his costume with slow reverence. The wind, now gone, leaves only stillness. Blackout.}
